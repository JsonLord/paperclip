# BPW 10-Step Pitch Compiler Review — 2026-09-18

## Native and canonical mapping

FounderOS registers `compile-pitch` under the native **Pitch Compilation** Project after accepted `compile-business-plan`, and reuses the canonical `pitch-agent` role. The `pitch.bpw-10-step` resource preserves `bpw-pitch-deck-10-step`, pitch storytelling, BPW evidence discipline, market, BMC, and finance identifiers. The source repository contains the registered sequence but no inspectable official visual template; therefore no official template compliance is claimed.

## Deck, claims, charts, and story

The canonical sequence is Mission, Problem, Solution, Defensibility (3B), Competition, Market, Metrics & Validation, Business Model, Go-to-Market, Team, and Status & Future. `PITCH.yaml` remains authoritative; Markdown is a concise slide specification. Each slide has one headline, at most five support points, claim IDs, structured charts, visual direction, separate notes, questions, and readiness.

Claims preserve observed/calculated/forecast/assumption/hypothesis classification, evidence/source artifacts, calculations, assumptions, contradictions, freshness, confidence, and maturity. Numeric claims resolve to canonical market/financial metrics. Funding asks equal the Financial Plan capital requirement or remain absent. Capabilities, team, partners, traction, testimonials, logos, and customer disclosure remain tied to registered facts and authorization.

The reusable `pitch.framework` resource is deliberately separate from the `pitch.bpw-10-step` layout. It persists the audience decision, evidence-backed thesis, belief ladder, narrative arc, evidence-driven archetype, slide-purpose map, objection coverage, ask, and configurable delivery plan. Every slide therefore answers an audience question, advances a belief, resolves or exposes an objection, and leaves one supported takeaway. The framework changes selection, emphasis, explanation, and density—not truth or canonical BPW ordering.

Charts retain type, metric, dimensions, actual data points, unit, time range, source/calculation IDs, annotation, and rendering guidance. A chart without provenance fails; insufficient data means no chart. Stitch and future PPTX/PDF/HTML renderers may style validated content but cannot select facts or perform business arithmetic.

## Validation, Firm, Hermes, and rendering

Deterministic validation enforces ordering, claim resolution, belief and thesis references, slide-purpose participation, density, chart provenance, numeric reconciliation, current/planned distinctions, identity/privacy, and the financial ask before Firm and Hermes. Hermes/Pitch Agent may improve emphasis and transitions only after these gates. The canonical outputs are `PITCH.yaml`, `PITCH.md`, `PITCH_CLAIM_MAP.yaml`, `PITCH_FRAMEWORK.yaml`, `OBJECTION_MAP.yaml`, `Q_AND_A.md`, `PITCH_RISKS.md`, and `READINESS.yaml`. Rendering is truthfully `NOT_RENDERED` until a compatible renderer/template produces and validates a real deck.

## Tests, limitations, and next slice

Focused tests cover native mapping, canonical order, unresolved claims, density, fake chart data, finance/ask reconciliation, product/team/privacy integrity, staleness, artifacts, Firm-before-Hermes ordering, and idempotency. The implementation does not fabricate design assets, logos, testimonials, or an official BPW template. The next primary decision slice should red-team the accumulated evidence and synthesis for the Build / Scale decision; Kickstarter and broad content automation remain separate optional systems.
