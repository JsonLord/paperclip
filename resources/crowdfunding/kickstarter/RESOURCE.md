# Kickstarter / Crowdfunding

A durable, platform-neutral operating pack for deciding whether crowdfunding fits, reconciling campaign economics, compiling an evidence-backed story from the Pitch Framework, reusing Content/GTM for prelaunch, governing manual launch, and learning only from verified market behavior.

This pack never implies that Kickstarter is suitable and never supplies current platform rules. Verify rules, fees, taxes, shipping, account state, and platform operations at execution time.
