# Block guidance

- Segments: use ICP/JTBD, qualification, commitment, usage, and retention; distinguish user, buyer, decision maker, and beneficiary only with evidence.
- Value propositions: reconcile problem, offer, behavior, commitment, and realized outcomes.
- Channels and relationships: include observed, tested, or explicit hypotheses only.
- Revenue and cost: reconcile to commercial payment and unit-economics state.
- Activities/resources/partners: describe the company's operating model, not FounderOS orchestration; distinguish current/required/hypothesized and actual/dependency/proposed/hypothesized.
